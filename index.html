01 / ABOUT

Composer &
Cellist

Aryanna Becerra is a composer and cellist pursuing a degree in Music Composition at the University of Utah. Her work explores the intersection of classical music and her Mexican and Argentine heritage, drawing inspiration from Latin American folklore, culture, and musical traditions.

As she develops her voice as a composer and performer, Aryanna hopes to create music that is both deeply personal and meaningful to the communities and stories that inspire her.
